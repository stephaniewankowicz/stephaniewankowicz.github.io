Computational Structural Biology

Stephanie A. Wankowicz

Assistant Professor · Vanderbilt University

I am a computational structural biologist developing algorithms to model protein conformational ensembles from X-ray crystallography and cryo-EM data. My work reveals how protein dynamics and entropy govern biological function, specificity, and drug binding.

I lead the Wankowicz Lab in the Department of Molecular Physiology and Biophysics, the Center for AI in Protein Dynamics, and the Center for Structural Biology at Vanderbilt University.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

Using computational methods to understand how protein motion shapes function, with the goal of transforming structural biology and drug design.

🔬

Conformational Ensemble Modeling

Developing automated tools (qFit) to extract low-populated but biologically important protein states from X-ray crystallography and cryo-EM data.

⚡

Protein Dynamics & Entropy

Investigating how conformational entropy is spatially redistributed during ligand binding and catalysis, linking dynamics to thermodynamics.

🧬

Macromolecular Specificity

Exploring how protein and water dynamics interplay to shape substrate recognition, with implications for enzymatic specificity and drug selectivity.

💊

Ligand & Protein Engineering

Applying ensemble-based insights to revolutionize targeted ligand and protein design by accounting for the full thermodynamic landscape.

🤖

AI in Structural Biology

Integrating machine learning with structural bioinformatics to uncover patterns in protein dynamics at scale across the Protein Data Bank.

📂

Open Science & Reproducibility

Advocating for open-source software, transparent data sharing, and reproducible methods in structural biology and AI-driven research.

The Tortured Proteins Department

A podcast co-hosted with James Fraser covering the latest in structural biology, protein dynamics, scientific policy, and life in academia.
